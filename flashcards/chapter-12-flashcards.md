## Chapter 12: Full Exam Simulation and Test-Taking Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Databricks ML Associate exam: total questions and time limit? | 45 questions, 90 minutes. Exam tip: budget ~2 minutes per question, leaving buffer for flagged review. |
| 2 | Domain weight: Databricks Machine Learning | 38% — the single largest domain. Prioritize securing these points if time runs short. |
| 3 | Domain weight: Model Development | 31% — second largest. Combined with Databricks ML, these two domains = 69% of the exam. |
| 4 | Domain weight: ML Workflows | 19% of the exam. |
| 5 | Domain weight: Model Deployment | 12% — smallest domain weight, but still worth full effort since no per-domain threshold exists. |
| 6 | Is there a minimum passing score required PER domain? | No. Only the overall weighted score across all 45 questions determines pass/fail. Exam trap: assuming each domain needs its own 70%. |
| 7 | Best strategy when 2 of 4 answer options both seem correct? | Re-check each against EVERY stated constraint (cost, scale, latency, governance). The distractor usually satisfies only some constraints. |
| 8 | Should you leave an uncertain question blank or guess? | Always guess and flag. No penalty for wrong answers — a guess has non-zero expected value, a blank guarantees zero. |
| 9 | Pacing check: at question 20, what elapsed time means you're on schedule? | 40 minutes (2 min/question × 20). Less than 40 min = ahead of pace; more = behind pace. |
| 10 | Data drift vs. concept drift — what's the difference? | Data drift = input feature distribution changes. Concept drift = the relationship between inputs and target label changes. Exam frequently swaps these terms as distractors. |
| 11 | Model decay vs. data drift vs. concept drift — which is the general term? | Model decay is the general/umbrella term for performance degradation over time; data drift and concept drift are specific causes tested separately on the exam. |
| 12 | Final-week review checklist priority order? | 1) Re-review highest-weighted domains (Databricks ML 38%, Model Development 31%) 2) Redo missed practice questions 3) Drill terminology traps (drift types, tracking vs. registry, etc.) 4) Do one full timed 90-minute simulation. |
